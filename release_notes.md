### Media Player Pro v1.1

Modern, high-performance media player based on [mpv](https://github.com/mpv-player/mpv).

#### Features:
- **Frosted Glass Skin**: Non-obstructive floating frosted glass bottom dock with glowing amber progress bar and scrubber.
- **Precision Navigation**: 7-second backward (`-7s`) and forward (`+7s`) skip buttons around the primary Play/Pause button.
- **Speed Play Dropup**: Instant playback rate switching with `0.25x`, `0.5x`, `1.0x (Normal)`, `1.25x`, `1.5x`, `2x`, `2.5x`, and `3x` presets.
- **Smooth Volume Slider**: Bottom-right volume track with interactive handle, percentage readout, and mouse wheel control.
- **Auto Check for Updates**: Automatically checks for new versions on GitHub Releases and opens the download page on click.
- **Cursor-Targeted Zoom & Pan**:
  - `Ctrl + Mouse Wheel`: Zoom into the exact part of the video where the mouse pointer is located.
  - `Ctrl + Left Drag`: Pan the video viewport to center any zoomed-in region.
  - `Ctrl + 0` / Middle Click: Reset zoom and pan.
- **3 Genuine Video Enhancement Levels**:
  - **Level 1 (Balanced)**: Deband + AMD FidelityFX Contrast-Adaptive Sharpening (CAS 0.35).
  - **Level 2 (High Clarity)**: Deep Deband + CAS 0.60 + High-quality Lanczos scaling.
  - **Level 3 (Ultra Fidelity)**: Ultra Deband + CAS 0.85 + Super-Resolution anti-ringing.
- **3-Second Autohide**: Controls and mouse pointer automatically hide after 3 seconds of non-touch during playback.
